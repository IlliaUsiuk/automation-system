## Hypothesis
Supplax runs a growing number of internal automations, but there's no single place to
see what they do, who owns them, or whether they're actually paying off. This
dashboard makes each automation's ROI (time/money saved) and status visible in one
place, so (a) leadership can justify investing in more automation work with real
numbers instead of anecdotes, and (b) employees/owners have a transparent view of
what's running, who's responsible, and what breaks.

## How We'll Measure It
Per-automation ROI card showing time and/or money saved vs. the manual process it
replaced — sourced from whatever baseline the automation's owner records at
registration time. See `docs/roi_explained.md` for the full methodology, assumptions,
and caveats behind this number.

## Qualitative Value
- Transparency for leadership and employees into what automation work exists and its
  effect, in one place instead of scattered across GitHub/ClickUp/tribal knowledge.
- Faster incident awareness once Telegram alerts ship (Next) — automation owners find
  out an automation broke instead of it silently failing.
- Cost visibility once token tracking ships (Later) — token spend becomes a visible
